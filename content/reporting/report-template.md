Copy the outline into your document editor and replace the labels with your own
observations. Use the official objectives and keep the draft in local storage you
control. The [report checklist](reporting/report#package-and-upload) covers export and upload.

## Cover and scope

| Field | Fill in |
| --- | --- |
| Report identity | Candidate OSID and exam date |
| Assessment window | Start/end and time zone |
| Scope | Targets and objectives from the control panel |
| Target order | List targets in the order they appear below |
| Results | Access and objectives actually demonstrated, with confidence and gaps |

## Repeat for each target

1. **Target identity** - label, address, date/time and objective.
2. **Observations** - relevant service, environment, and what established it.
3. **Timeline** - ordered actions actually performed, execution location, the
   identity in use, and the output that supports each result.
4. **Access and privilege** - separate initial and final identities and explain
   the evidence for each. Show dependencies between systems when they matter.
5. **Proof** - caption the screenshot with figure ID, target address and objective.
   Keep the required proof and address legible in the same image.
6. **Sources** - original reference URLs, versions, and reasoning for any changes
   you made. Follow OffSec's instructions for including modified material.

## Evidence inventory

| Figure | Target | Observation | Check before export |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Figure number | Target label and address | Objective or result the image supports | Read at normal PDF zoom and confirm the caption points to the correct target |

Reference figures from the paragraph that relies on them. Keep multiple hosts in
an AD set independently labeled. A screenshot of one host does not establish an
objective on another host. Record failures as observations and mark unproven
results clearly. Share the report only through the designated submission channel.
