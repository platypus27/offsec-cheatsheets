## Before the exam

- [ ] Open the offline build and verify notes, prepared files and report template.
- [ ] Recheck the current guide, tool restrictions and proctoring setup.

## Time plan

The guide verified on 2026-11-18 allows 23h45m of exam access, then 24h for report
upload. Record both deadlines. OffSec says scaled scoring applies to starts from
December 1, 2026 at 08:00 GMT. Use the objectives in your control panel and the
[official scoring guidance](https://help.offsec.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040165632-OSCP-Exam-Guide).

## Tool restrictions

Before the sitting, read the tool restrictions in the [official exam guide](https://help.offsec.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040165632-OSCP-Exam-Guide).
The guide restricts spoofing, commercial tools, automatic exploitation, mass
vulnerability scanners and AI chatbots. It applies the restrictions to individual
features as well as standalone tools. Metasploit and Meterpreter have their own
target and usage rules. Review those rules for your sitting before choosing any
tool with restricted features.

## Control panel and contact

Use the exam control panel to check each target's objectives and record proof.
Targets start freshly reverted. Reverting a target during the exam discards
local changes, so save evidence first and track the remaining reverts shown in
the panel. Report VPN or target connectivity issues promptly in proctoring chat.

## Breaks

- [ ] Save notes and record the next action before stepping away.
- [ ] Follow proctor instructions for breaks and returning.

## Submission

- [ ] Recheck [proof screenshots](reporting/report#evidence), report completeness and candidate-specific packaging.
- [ ] Confirm the upload and required verification steps before the deadline.

See the [package and upload checklist](reporting/report#package-and-upload) and
the [official OSCP+ guide](https://help.offsec.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040165632-OSCP-Exam-Guide).
