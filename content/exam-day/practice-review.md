Use this with your authorized exercises and Lab notebook. Keep your own practice
evidence separate from course solutions and personal credentials.

## Plan a module session

1. Choose a topic in the [study map](field-guide/study-map#course-topic-map).
2. Describe the expected behavior in one sentence. Write down what you would
   need to observe to be sure.
3. Record exercise name, date, environment and versions. Keep raw screenshots
   and documents outside the browser.
4. Answer an original review question from memory before reopening your notes.
   Record which concept you could explain and which still needs practice.

## Review a challenge session

| Record | Question to answer |
| --- | --- |
| Baseline | What could you verify at the start, and from where? |
| Evidence | Which observation changed your judgment? Where did you save it? |
| Time spent | Where did you repeat work without gaining new information? |
| Help used | Did you complete the objective independently, with a hint, or after a walkthrough? |
| Next practice objective | Which missing concept or documentation habit can you rehearse in isolation? |

When reviewing multiple targets, use separate notebook entries. Record the
exercise name in the notes if target IPs are reused between lab sessions.

## Rehearse submission

Use a fictional target and screenshots with no private data to fill the
[report outline](reporting/report-template). Export a PDF and check the document
at ordinary zoom. Rehearse the archive and local checksum separately from the
actual submission portal. Review the candidate's instructions before exam day.
